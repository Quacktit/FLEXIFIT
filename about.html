Built by lifters,

FLEXIFIT started as a single 1,200 sq. ft. room with two power racks. Eight years and three landlords later, it's an 11,000 sq. ft. floor — because the people who trained here kept bringing someone with them.

Our story

From one power rack to a full floor

FLEXIFIT was opened in 2016 by a small group of competitive powerlifters who were tired of chain gyms where nobody corrected your form and every machine had a six-person queue. The idea was simple: put a coach on the floor at all times, keep the equipment in working order, and never let membership numbers outgrow the space.

That approach meant slower growth than most gyms in Vadodara — we moved locations twice and expanded three times, always waiting until the floor could genuinely support more members before opening enrolment further. Today the gym runs strength, conditioning, boxing and personal training programs out of one dedicated facility on Gotri Road.

Milestones

How we got here

2016

First location opens

A 1,200 sq. ft. room on Old Padra Road with two squat racks, a bench, and a founding batch of 40 members.

2018

Move to Gotri Road

Relocated to a 4,500 sq. ft. space to add a dedicated conditioning zone and the first group class timetable.

2020

Boxing rig installed

Added a boxing and bag-work section, plus outdoor-style sled and prowler training on the back deck.

2022

Personal training desk launches

Brought on dedicated 1-on-1 coaches and started monthly InBody body-composition scans for members.

2024

Current 11,000 sq. ft. facility

Expanded into the current building — full strength floor, two group-class studios, boxing rig and a recovery corner.

What we stand for

Mission & values

M

Coached, not just supervised

A gym membership should come with actual coaching. Every class and peak open-gym hour has a coach on the floor watching form, not standing at a desk.

M

Capacity over crowding

We cap batch sizes and monitor floor density during peak hours instead of overselling memberships to hit a number.

M

Progress is measurable

Lift logs, attendance and monthly scans exist so members can see whether the program is actually working — not just how they feel that day.

The facility

Inside the 11,000 sq. ft. floor

Strength Zone

8 competition platforms, full plate range up to 25kg, specialty bars and a dedicated warm-up rack.

Conditioning Studio

Sleds, prowlers, kettlebells, battle ropes and a rig built for circuit-style HIIT classes.

Boxing Rig

6 heavy bags, a speed-bag station and a padded sparring area for technique sessions.

Recovery Corner

Foam rollers, mobility bands, a stretching mat area and two massage chairs for post-session recovery.
