Five programs, one floor

Pick a goal.
The program follows.

Every program below runs on a fixed weekly timetable with a named coach. Drop into any class with an active membership, or lock in a personal training slot for one-on-one work.

Strength

Strength & Powerlifting

Squat, bench and deadlift progressions built around 4–6 week blocks, with a coach on every platform correcting bar path and bracing.

Level: Beginner to competitive Class length: 60 minutes Batches: Mon / Wed / Fri — 6 AM, 7 AM, 6 PM, 7 PM
Conditioning

HIIT Conditioning

Kettlebells, sleds and bodyweight circuits in 45-minute rounds designed to keep heart rate in zone for the full session.

Level: All levels Class length: 45 minutes Batches: Tue / Thu / Sat — 6 AM, 7 AM, 6 PM
Combat

Boxing & Bag Work

Footwork, combinations and pad rounds on the boxing rig. Builds conditioning without a single burpee.

Level: All levels Class length: 50 minutes Batches: Mon / Wed / Fri — 8 AM, 8 PM
Functional

Functional Fitness

Mobility, core and movement-pattern work aimed at older adults and anyone returning from injury or a long break.

Level: Beginner-friendly Class length: 40 minutes Batches: Tue / Thu / Sat — 9 AM
1-on-1

Personal Training

A dedicated coach, a written program and a monthly re-test. Slots are booked individually around your schedule.

Level: Custom to you Session length: 60 minutes Batches: By appointment, 5 AM – 10 PM daily
Open Gym

Open Floor Access

Full access to the strength zone and conditioning studio outside class hours, with a coach present at all peak times.

Level: All levels Access: Included with every membership Hours: Mon–Sat 5 AM–11 PM · Sun 6 AM–2 PM
