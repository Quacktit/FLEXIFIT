Common questions

FAQs

Is the first session really free?

Yes — one free open-gym or class session for any first-time visitor, no card details required. Book it through the membership page.

Do you offer day passes?

Yes, day passes are available for ₹299 at the front desk for anyone not ready to commit to a monthly plan yet.

Can I freeze my membership?

Memberships can be frozen for up to 30 days a year for travel or medical reasons — just message the front desk in advance.

Is parking available?

Yes, free two-wheeler and car parking is available in the Yash Business Hub basement for all members.
