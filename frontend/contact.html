Common questions

FAQs

Do you offer a trial session?

Yes. Your first visit is a complimentary 1-on-1 assessment and workout with a coach. No open-gym chaos, just a dedicated session to see if our private studio is the right fit for you. Booking in advance is required.

Can I drop in or train without a coach?

No. To guarantee zero wait times and complete privacy for our clients, we do not offer day passes or general open-gym access. We are a 100% coaching-focused, appointment-only facility.

What if I need to travel or take a break?

We understand life happens. Memberships can be paused for up to 30 days a year for travel or medical reasons. Just let your coach know in advance so we can adjust your plan.

Is parking provided at the studio?

Yes. Free two-wheeler and car parking is available in the Yash Business Hub basement for all members, so you can arrive stress-free for your booked session.
