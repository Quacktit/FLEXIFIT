Our story

30 years in the industry, one focused room

FLEXIFIT was founded by Rahul Makwana with a single vision: to create a premium, completely focused training environment. With over 30 years of hands-on experience in the fitness industry and a highly successful commercial gym currently operating at another location, Rahul knew exactly what was missing in the market—an exclusive space where beginners and elite athletes alike get uninterrupted, expert guidance.

What we stand for

Mission & values

Coached, not just supervised

Every session has a coach on the floor watching your form for the full hour — not standing at a desk between check-ins.

One coach, one client, one hour

We don't run batches or group classes. Every slot on the schedule belongs to a single client and their coach.

Progress is measurable

Lift logs and regular check-ins exist so you can see whether the program is actually working — not just how you feel that day.

The facility

Inside the 800+ sq. ft. studio

Strength Zone

A full free-weight setup — barbells, plates, smith machine, racks and benches — the same kind of equipment you'd find on a commercial gym floor, minus the crowd.

Conditioning Zone

SkiErg, RowErg, tyre flips and sledge hammers for full-body conditioning work.

Functional Training

Medicine balls, yoga balls and mobility tools for bodyweight, functional and rehab-friendly sessions.

Boxing Corner

A punching bag for technique work, combinations and conditioning rounds.
