This policy covers flexifitgym.in only — the contact and membership application forms on this site. It does not cover in-gym paperwork, waivers, or InBody scan records you complete on-site, which are handled separately by our front desk.

FLEXIFIT Gym ("FLEXIFIT", "we", "us", "our") operates this website. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you use it, why we collect it, and what choices you have. By submitting a form on this site, you agree to the practices described below.

01Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to submit through our website forms. We do not require account creation or login to browse the site.

Contact Form

When you use the form on our Contact page, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Subject / reason for enquiry
  • Your message

Membership Application Form

When you apply to join through our Membership page, we collect:

  • Full name, email address and phone number
  • The plan you selected
  • Your fitness goal
  • Your preferred start date
  • Any additional notes you choose to add

Automatically Collected Information

Our hosting and form-processing infrastructure may log standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type and page-visit timestamps for security and diagnostic purposes. We do not currently run analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

02How We Use It

We use the information you submit to:

  • Respond to enquiries sent through the contact form
  • Process and follow up on membership applications, including calling or emailing you to confirm plan details and your start date
  • Keep basic records of prospective and current members for front-desk and coaching purposes
  • Improve our website and services based on the enquiries we receive
  • Meet legal, accounting or regulatory obligations where applicable

We do not use the information you submit for automated decision-making or profiling, and we do not sell your personal information to anyone.

03Legal Basis for Processing

We process the personal information described above on the following bases:

  • Consent — you provide this information voluntarily by submitting a form
  • Contract — processing your membership details is necessary to set up and administer your gym membership
  • Legitimate interest — responding to enquiries and maintaining basic business records

04Cookies & Local Storage

This website does not set marketing or tracking cookies. Your browser may store minor, non-identifying technical preferences (for example, whether the mobile menu is open) locally on your device for the duration of your visit; this data never leaves your browser.

If we add analytics, advertising or chat-widget tools in future, we will update this section and, where required by law, request your consent first.

05Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We may share it only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — our website host and form-processing backend, solely to deliver your submission to our team
  • Internal staff — FLEXIFIT front-desk and membership staff, so they can respond to your enquiry or application
  • Legal requirements — where disclosure is required to comply with applicable law, a court order, or to protect the rights, property or safety of FLEXIFIT, our members or the public
  • Business transfers — if FLEXIFIT is ever involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to the same protections described here

06Data Storage & Security

Form submissions are transmitted over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our backend service and stored on our hosting provider's servers. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and any relevant authority as required by applicable law.

07Data Retention

We keep contact and membership enquiry records for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you, process your application, and maintain relevant business or legal records — typically no longer than 24 months from your last interaction with us, unless you become a member, in which case membership records are retained for the duration of your membership plus a reasonable period afterward. You can ask us to delete your information sooner; see "Your Rights" below.

08Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you and request a copy
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Ask us to delete your personal information
  • Withdraw consent for us to contact you at any time
  • Object to or restrict certain processing of your information

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in the "Contact Us" section below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

09Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 18. Junior members join through an in-person process with a parent or guardian, not through this website's forms. If you believe a minor has submitted personal information to us through the site, please contact us and we will delete it.

10Third-Party Links

Our footer and social icons may link to third-party sites such as Instagram, Facebook or YouTube. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites, and we encourage you to review their own privacy policies.

11Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will post the revised version on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the site after changes are posted means you accept the revised policy.
